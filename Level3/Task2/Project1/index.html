01 / THE PROGRAM

An opportunity to learn beyond the classroom.

The Web Developer Internship at Cognifyz Technologies focuses on practical learning through development assignments and projects. It is designed for students, freshers and aspiring developers looking to strengthen their web development foundations.

Participants may work with frontend technologies, build responsive interfaces, improve problem-solving skills and develop projects that demonstrate their abilities.
